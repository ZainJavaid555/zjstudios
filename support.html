How to play

Drag your finger across the top of the screen to aim, then let go to drop a dinosaur. When two dinosaurs of the same kind touch, they merge into the next one up the chain.

There are fourteen dinosaurs, starting small and ending with the T-Rex. The tank fills as you play, so leave yourself room. If it overflows, the run ends and you start again.

Powerups

  • Hook pulls one dinosaur out of the pile.
  • Upgrade turns one dinosaur into the next one up.
  • Shake rattles the whole tank so everything settles.
  • Remove Smallest clears out the smallest dinosaurs to make space.

You can claim up to three powerups free in each run. They are free, and there is nothing to buy.

Common questions

Does the game need the internet?

No. Dino Merge works fully offline. The only time it looks for a connection is to check the date for daily rewards.

Are there ads?

No. This version has no advertising at all.

Are there any purchases?

No. The game is free and every feature in it is free.

I lost my high score

Scores are saved on your device only. Deleting the app removes them, and there is no way to bring them back. We do not keep a copy on a server.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?

Not at the moment. Because everything is stored on your device and there is no account, progress stays on the phone it was made on.

The game is slow or crashes

Close the app fully and reopen it. If that does not help, restart your phone. If it still happens, email us with your iPhone model and iOS version and we will look into it.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Open the settings button on the main menu. Music, sound effects and vibration each have their own switch.
