Nutty Stacks Privacy Policy

Color sort puzzle by ZJ Studios

Effective 29 October 2026

This policy explains how Nutty Stacks (version 1.0 and later) for iPhone and iPad, made by ZJ Studios, handles your information. The short version: we do not collect any of it.

Information we collect

None. Nutty Stacks does not ask for your name, email address or any account, and it does not collect personal information or usage data.

Data saved on your device

The game keeps a small amount of data in its own private storage on your device so you can continue where you left off:

  • Stars earned and levels unlocked in each mode
  • The last level you played
  • Your sound and music settings

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising and tracking

Nutty Stacks has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use the advertising identifier.

Internet use

The game works fully offline and does not send anything over the internet.

Third parties

We do not share any data with anyone. Apple may collect download and purchase information under its own privacy policy.

Children

Nutty Stacks is suitable for everyone. Because we collect nothing, we collect nothing from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, we will update this page and its effective date.
