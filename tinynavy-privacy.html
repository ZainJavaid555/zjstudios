Tiny Navy Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Tiny Navy: Sea Battle io does with information on your device. The short version is that the game keeps your progress on your device and sends nothing about you anywhere.

Effective date
15 October 2026
Applies to
Tiny Navy: Sea Battle io for iPhone and iPad, version 1.0
Published by
ZJ Studios

In plain words

We do not ask for your real name, your email address or any account. We do not show advertising in the game. There are no in app purchases. We do not use any analytics or tracking service. Nothing about you or your device leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Tiny Navy collects no personal information and no usage information. There is no account, no sign up and no login.

What the game saves on your device

The game keeps a small save in its own private storage on your device. It holds:

  • Your coins, your ship level and your upgrades
  • The captain avatar and ship colours you picked
  • The captain callsign you typed in, if you changed it
  • Your best run, your daily orders and your daily supply streak
  • Whether you have already seen the first launch guide

That is the whole save. The callsign is a nickname you choose for fun and is shown only inside the game on your own device. The save is readable only by this game, it is never sent anywhere, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

There is none. The game contains no advertising network, no analytics library and no tracking software of any kind. We do not use the advertising identifier on your device and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Network use

The game works fully offline. All the ships you battle are controlled by the game on your device, not by other players, and it does not need an internet connection to run.

Other companies

We share no data with anyone, because we hold none. Apple provides the App Store and may collect its own information about downloads, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy and not by ours.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience. Because it collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Your choices

Since nothing is collected, there is nothing to request, correct or delete from us. If you want to remove your save, delete the app from your device and it goes with it.

Changes to this policy

This policy covers version 1.0. If a future version adds anything that changes what the game does with information, we will update this page and change the effective date at the top before that version is released.
