How to play

You captain a tiny boat on the open sea. Hold the game in landscape and drag on the screen to steer. Your guns fire on their own whenever an enemy ship is in range, so all you decide is where to sail.

Every ship shows its tier. Ships of a lower tier than yours are easy targets: sink them to collect coins. Ships of a higher tier hit much harder, so keep your distance until you have grown. When a big ship is close, a warning appears on the side of the screen.

Growing your ship

  • Bank your coins. The coins from a run are added when your ship sinks or when you head back to port.
  • Level up your ship in the shipyard to climb 10 tiers, from a Patrol Boat to a Flagship Carrier.
  • Upgrade your hull, engine, firepower and salvage for an extra edge.
  • Daily supply and daily orders give bonus coins every day.

Common questions

How do I pause or leave a run?

Tap the round pause button next to the map. You can resume, or go back to the shipyard. If you have sunk at least one ship, your coins from that run are kept when you leave.

Does the game need the internet?

No. Tiny Navy works fully offline. Every enemy ship is controlled by the game.

Are there ads?

No. There is no advertising anywhere in the game.

Are there any purchases?

No. There are no in app purchases. Everything is earned by playing.

Does my progress save?

Yes. Your coins, ship level, upgrades and choices are saved automatically on your device.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app removes it and there is no way to bring it back. We do not keep a copy on a server.

Can I move my progress to a new device?

Not at the moment. Because everything is stored on your device and there is no account, progress stays on the device it was made on.

How do I turn the sound down?

Use the volume buttons on your device, or the silent switch.

The game is slow or it crashes

Close the app fully and open it again. If that does not help, restart your device. If it still happens, email us with your device model and your iOS version and we will look into it.

Privacy

Tiny Navy collects no personal information. There is no account and no sign up. Our privacy policy explains exactly what the game saves and where it stays.
