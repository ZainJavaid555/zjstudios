How to play

Tap a bolt to lift the top nut, then tap another bolt to drop it there. Sort the nuts so every bolt holds a single color.

  • Relaxed mode: any nut can go on any bolt that has space. Finish before your turns run out.
  • Classic mode: a nut can only go on the same color or on an empty bolt. Plan ahead!

Each mode has 500 levels that go from easy to medium to hard. Solve a level in fewer moves to earn up to 3 stars.

Common questions

Do I need the internet to play?
No. Nutty Stacks works fully offline.

I made a mistake. Can I take it back?
Yes. Use the undo button at the top of the screen, or restart the level at any time.

Where is my progress saved?
Your stars and unlocked levels are saved on your device. If you delete the app, your progress is removed too.

How do I turn off the music or sound?
Open Settings from the home screen, or pause during a level, and use the Sound and Music switches.
