How to play

You and your AI partner play 2 vs 2 padel against two rivals.

  • Move: drag one finger on the court to run.
  • Hit: swipe up with another finger when the ball comes to you.
  • Serve: swipe up when the power bar is in the green zone for a strong serve.
  • Use the glass: the ball can bounce off the walls and stay in play, just like real padel.

Win points to win games, and win games to win the match. Play Quick Matches or climb the World Tour, earn coins and gems, upgrade your player and unlock new looks in the Locker.

Common questions

Do I need the internet to play?
No. Padel Squad works fully offline.

Are there ads or purchases?
No. There are no ads and no in app purchases. Coins and gems are earned by playing.

Where is my progress saved?
Your progress is saved on your device. If you delete the app, your progress is removed too.

How do I turn off the music, sound or vibration?
Open Settings from the gear button on the home screen, or pause during a match.

My phone gets warm. What can I do?
Turn on Battery Saver in Settings. The game will run cooler and use less battery.
