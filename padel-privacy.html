Padel Squad Privacy Policy

2v2 padel game by ZJ Studios

Effective 3 November 2026

This policy explains how Padel Squad (version 1.0 and later) for iPhone and iPad, made by ZJ Studios, handles your information. The short version: we do not collect any of it.

Information we collect

None. Padel Squad does not ask for your name, email address or any account, and it does not collect personal information or usage data.

Data saved on your device

The game keeps a small amount of data in its own private storage on your device so you can continue where you left off:

  • Your coins, gems, level and player upgrades
  • Your player's look and the items you have unlocked
  • World Tour progress, daily rewards, goals and awards
  • Your sound, music, vibration and battery saver settings

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

Padel Squad has no in app purchases. Coins and gems are earned by playing and have no real money value.

Advertising and tracking

Padel Squad has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use the advertising identifier.

Internet use

The game works fully offline and does not send anything over the internet.

Third parties

We do not share any data with anyone. Apple may collect download information under its own privacy policy.

Children

Padel Squad is suitable for everyone. Because we collect nothing, we collect nothing from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, for example by adding online play, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
